The Stylophone S-1 plays one note at a time and one voice at a time. StyloPoly samples all three of its voices and lets you play them together, polyphonically, with the pops and crackles of the stylus available as separate attack and release noise layers.

Alongside the mixer sit an ADSR envelope, vibrato with depth and rate, roughly matching the original when both are centred, and two filters: a highpass from 20 Hz to 2 kHz and a lowpass from 200 Hz to 22 kHz, both with resonance. The output is either the line out or the instrument's internal speaker miked with a Shure SM57. Mono and Static switches put the original's behaviour back whenever you want it.

A tape echo and a room reverb finish the chain, both from impulse responses. The preset list runs from faithful Stylophone tones to sounds the hardware cannot make, and a second mode stretches the samples far above and below the original range.

163 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored as lossless FLAC in one sample file that the installer puts in place.

## Features

- All three Stylophone voices, blendable together in one mixer.
- Polyphonic, or Mono for the original's one-note-at-a-time behaviour.
- Separate attack and release stylus noise layers.
- ADSR envelope and LFO vibrato with depth and rate.
- Highpass filter (20 Hz to 2 kHz) and lowpass filter (200 Hz to 22 kHz), both with resonance.
- Line output or the internal speaker miked with a Shure SM57.
- Tape echo and room reverb impulse responses.
- Pitch Stretched mode extending the instrument far beyond its original range.
- Preset list running from faithful to far beyond the hardware.
- 163 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored losslessly (73 MB source library).
